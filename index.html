// ReasonateAI · Dual-Twin Architecture

Every change gets a rehearsal before it goes on stage.

You describe what you want. Three AI agents plan it, write it and test it inside a private twin of your app. Only what passes every check gets promoted to the live twin — with zero downtime. After launch, watchers keep an eye on the live app and send any drift back into the loop to be fixed.
